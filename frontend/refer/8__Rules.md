# Document 8: Rules.md — Non-Negotiable System & Safety Rules
## [REVISION 1.1 — POST-AUDIT HARDENED]

> **Revision note:** Closes SAFE-004, XC-010, and embeds the audit's P0/P1 findings as binding rules in the one document whose stated purpose is "non-negotiable." Two of the five G&SR rules below previously had no data model and no enforcement point anywhere in the schema — they could not actually be checked by Sentinel as written. This revision names the required enforcement point for each.

## 1. Indian Railways Operational & Safety Rules (G&SR)

- **Absolute Block Exclusion:** A track block section placed under an active maintenance block must not be assigned to any moving passenger or freight train under any circumstances. **Enforcement point:** CP-SAT `NoOverlap` constraint per section (TechSpec.md §2) plus Sentinel structural re-check at solve-time and again at T−2h transmission time.
- **Deterministic Interlocking Precedence:** Interlocking safety logic always supersedes maintenance demands. Signal disconnections must be formally acknowledged by the Station Master and Chief Controller prior to work commencement. **Enforcement point (SAFE-004 fix — previously unenforceable):** `operations.signal_acknowledgments` (Schema.md §2) requires both `sm_acked_at` and `controller_acked_at` to be populated before a Signal & Telecom demand's plan may transition past `SENTINEL_PASSED`; Sentinel check G&SR-2 verifies this directly.
- **Fail-Closed Default State:** If any communication link, solver module, or data feed fails, all active authorizations must remain in their last verified safe state. The system must never grant a maintenance block under uncertain telemetry. **Enforcement point:** staleness TTL on all ingested feeds (`source_ingested_at`, Schema.md §2); IMD weather feed defaults to deferring outdoor high-risk work when stale or unavailable (AppFlow.md Scenario C), not to assuming clear conditions.
- **Power Isolation Boundaries:** Traction power shutdowns (TRD) must match physical OHE elementary feeding sections and isolator switch boundaries to prevent electrical back-feeding from adjacent lines. **Enforcement point (SAFE-004 fix — previously unenforceable):** `infrastructure.ohe_feeding_sections` and `infrastructure.section_feeding_map` (Schema.md §2) give Sentinel check G&SR-4 a concrete boundary set to verify a TRD plan's section coverage against; without this data model, this rule could not be checked by anything and any TRD block passed by default.
- **Headway Margins:** Scheduled block windows must preserve minimum 15-minute headway buffers before the arrival of high-priority passenger trains (e.g., Vande Bharat, Rajdhani). **Enforcement point:** headway-expanded intervals in the `NoOverlap` constraint (TechSpec.md §2); re-verified at T−2h against COA_LIVE/RTIS for unscheduled specials not present in WTT/FOIS at solve-time.

## 2. AI Safety & Mathematical Boundaries

- **Advisory Scope of Machine Learning:** Machine learning models (PyTorch / XGBoost) are strictly restricted to parameter estimation (asset failure risk, freight path density, weather delay factors). They never appear in a feasibility constraint — only in objective coefficients ($\Pi_k$, $\rho_f$) that a deterministic solver then optimizes over.
- **Prohibition of Direct AI Dispatch:** No neural network or LLM is permitted to directly grant, modify, or clear track block authorities. All scheduling decisions must be computed via deterministic MILP/CP-SAT solvers.
- **Mandatory Sentinel Verification:** Every generated block plan — including emergency `PROVISIONAL` plans — must pass through the independent, rule-based Sentinel verification module before being rendered on user consoles. Sentinel is a **validator**, never an **executor**: it does not itself revoke, grant, or transmit anything (see TechSpec.md ADR-004, ADR-006).
- **Human-in-the-Loop Authority:** The system provides decision support; formal block locking and dispatch require explicit digital approval from a Sr. DOM and a **distinct** DRM (server-enforced: `decided_by ≠ authorized_by`, Schema.md §2). Even under P0 emergency timing, the plan is `PROVISIONAL` until the Chief Controller acknowledges it — the Controller's existing legal dispatch authority under G&SR is never bypassed.
- **Model Calibration Transparency:** Every ML-derived estimate ($\Pi_k$, $\rho_f$, weather penalty coefficients) must be presented with a sensitivity/confidence indication (e.g., schedule stability under ±20% perturbation), not as a bare number, since Sentinel checks structural validity, not the plausibility of the numbers a model produced.

## 3. Financial & Operational Integrity Standards

- **Standardized Punctuality Metrics:** Passenger delay penalties ($C_{\text{pax}}$) and freight detention costs ($C_{\text{frt}}$) must use standardized Railway Board penalty values.
- **Rigorous Baseline Benchmarking:** Baseline 1 ($B_1$) must represent an honestly tuned, competitive heuristic algorithm to prevent biased or artificial performance claims. **Concretely:** $B_1$'s parameters must be tuned via a documented protocol (grid-search on a held-out tuning split, distinct from the evaluation split) and the tuning methodology must be published alongside any comparative KPI figure — a KPI comparison against an untuned strawman baseline is not acceptable under this rule.
- **Dataset Honesty:** FR-024's benchmarking suite runs over a **26-week simulated scenario set with fixed random seeds** — not, as earlier drafts stated, a "historical dataset." No historical Indian Railways operational data exists anywhere in this project's pipeline. All comparative KPI figures (e.g., delay-minute or utilization deltas) are unpublishable as fact until they are actually measured by running $B_0$, $B_1$, and RAIL-BLOC on identical seeded scenarios.
- **Immutable Record Keeping:** All demand submissions, optimization outputs, administrative overrides, and field clearances must be permanently recorded in the append-only SHA-256 cryptographic ledger. **Terminology correction:** the ledger is **tamper-evident**, not "tamper-proof" — a hash chain makes unauthorized alteration detectable, it does not physically prevent a sufficiently privileged database role from attempting one. Mutation is additionally blocked at the database layer via an INSERT-only `ledger_writer` role and `BEFORE UPDATE/DELETE` guard triggers (Schema.md §2, DB-001 fix), which is the actual prevention mechanism.

## 4. Code Quality & Engineering Standards

- **Strict Type Safety:** All frontend code must use TypeScript with strict mode enabled (`noImplicitAny: true`). All API boundaries in Python must use Pydantic v2 schemas.
- **Spatial Query Optimization:** All PostGIS geospatial queries on hot paths must utilize spatial GiST indexes (`idx_block_sections_geom`, and the GiST range index on demand scheduling windows — PERF-002 fix).
- **Zero Hardcoding Policy:** Operational parameters, penalty coefficients, and database credentials must be managed via structured environment variables and configuration files. **Clarification (XC-010 fix):** this explicitly includes scheduling *cadences*, not only numeric coefficients — e.g., PRD.md FR-013's "Every Thursday 15:00 hrs" tactical-generation trigger must be stored as a configurable cron expression, not a literal hardcoded time, or it violates this rule by its own letter.

## 5. Zero-Tolerance Demonstration & Simulation Honesty Rules

- **Live Optimization Verification:** The demonstration must execute real mathematical optimization using Google OR-Tools in real-time; hardcoded or pre-recorded schedule solutions are strictly prohibited.
- **Explicit Data Labeling:** All synthetic railway network topologies, train paths, and sensor feeds must be explicitly labeled as simulated data in the Atlas console — concretely, a persistent, non-dismissible "SIMULATED DATA" watermark (Design.md §3), not a footnote.
- **Verifiable Cryptographic Proof:** The cryptographic hash chain in the audit ledger must be live-verifiable during evaluations via the `/api/v1/ledger/verify` endpoint.
- **No Unmeasured Claims Presented as Fact (NEW):** Any performance, reliability, or coverage figure not yet actually measured on the built system — uptime percentages, frame-rate figures, solver latency, "N/N rules verified" counts — must be labeled as a **target** until a real measurement exists, and the measurement method must be stated alongside the figure once it does. This rule exists because the pre-implementation Tracker.md previously marked 40/40 tasks "Completed & Verified" with evidence lines (e.g., "14/14 rules verified," "60 FPS," "≤30s solve") that could not have existed for a system with no code — see Tracker.md §0 for the corrected status baseline.

## 6. Post-Audit Hardening Requirements (Binding — NEW)

The following are elevated from audit findings to non-negotiable rules, since each closes a gap that would otherwise let an unverified or unsafe plan reach a human approver or COA:

- **R6.1 (SAFE-001):** The database migration must create the `pgcrypto` extension before the ledger trigger is created. A ledger that cannot compute a hash is not an optional degradation — it blocks all further approvals.
- **R6.2 (SAFE-002):** No plan may be approved, authorized, or transmitted unless its current `content_hash` matches the `sentinel_hash` recorded at its last `SENTINEL_PASSED` transition. Any content mutation after that point invalidates the hash and requires a new revision to re-enter the Sentinel chain.
- **R6.3 (APP-001):** The Sr. DOM and DRM on any single plan's approval chain must be distinct actors. This is enforced server-side (`chk_distinct_approvers`, Schema.md §2), not left as a UI convention.
- **R6.4 (SAFE-003):** No emergency re-plan, regardless of time pressure, may bypass Sentinel's structural checks. The 45-second NFR-002 budget is met by scoping the solve to the affected corridor, not by skipping verification.
- **R6.5 (SAFE-004):** A TRD (traction power) block plan must be rejected by Sentinel if its section set is not fully coverable by `infrastructure.section_feeding_map` boundaries. A Signal & Telecom block plan must be rejected if `operations.signal_acknowledgments` lacks both a Station Master and Controller acknowledgment.
- **R6.6 (DOC-001):** No task, feature, or safety check may be marked complete/verified in Tracker.md without a corresponding, runnable test whose result is the evidence cited.
